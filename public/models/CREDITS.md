These aircraft models are used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0

permits commercial use provided the original author is credited.

- **Boeing 787-9** — based on "Boeing 787-9" by Nobilis 2
  (https://sketchfab.com/nobilishornet2), from
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/boeing-787-9-b6711e2e698e4e469675c1154a50b7a3.
  Retinted to the Citadelle livery.
- **Airbus A330-900neo** — based on "Airbus_a330-900_neo" by Ahmed Mahdi
  (https://sketchfab.com/mhmdshwatahmdmhdy922), from
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/airbus-a330-900-neo-0cc4dcda3db8459687138e8dde33ac9d.
  Retinted to the Citadelle livery.
- **Airbus A350-900** — geometry from a Sketchfab download; the archive did
  not include the license file present for the other models. Re-confirm the
  license on the original Sketchfab listing before relying on this
  attribution alone. Retinted to the Citadelle livery.
- **Airbus A320neo** — based on "A320neo" by pranav27
  (https://sketchfab.com/pranav27), from
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/a320neo-b14863d4091e41e6abe25832a0af3b00.
  Used as provided (real-world paint scheme), except the modeled "AIRBUS"
  and "neo" wordmark geometry is hidden at render time.
- **Airbus A321neo** — based on "Germanwings+A320" by mendezbrahian590
  (https://sketchfab.com/mendezbrahian590), from
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/germanwingsa320-e0dbb4a8e7ee4447ad2b930a73cb1e80.
  Used entirely as provided, including the original Germanwings livery and
  wordmark baked into its textures — kept at explicit user request despite
  this being a real third-party airline's branding, not Citadelle's own.
- **Boeing 737-800** — geometry/textures from a Sketchfab download; the
  archive did not include a license file. Re-confirm the license on the
  original Sketchfab listing. Used entirely as provided, including the
  "BOEING 737-800" wordmark baked into its texture.

For 320/321/738, geometry and UVs are otherwise unmodified from the
downloaded source; only texture resolution was reduced for web delivery
(no content changes). For 789/330/350, base color materials were also
retinted to the Citadelle Airlines livery (see
`src/components/GltfAirplaneModel.tsx`).
